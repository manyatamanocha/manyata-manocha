How I work

  1. 01

    Pick one narrow problem

    Adhikaar covers one asset after a death, the bank deposit, and leaves out seven others I could not verify in time.

  2. 02

    Talk to people before choosing a user

    For Vantage I ran 9 interviews across 8 roles. The persona came out of the interviews.

  3. 03

    Write the PRD with the evidence labelled

    Each finding says whether a person said it, I think it, or it is still an assumption.

  4. 04

    Build and ship it myself

    Claude Code, Next.js, Supabase and the Groq API, with automated tests: 53 in Adhikaar, 268 in Vantage.

  5. 05

    Measure honestly, then change the product

    Only 2 of 17 Vantage testers found the scenario quiz, so I changed the navigation.
