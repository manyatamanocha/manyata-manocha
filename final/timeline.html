My Journey to Product

  1. 012016

    I studied commerce.

    University of Delhi, India.

    I chose commerce because of my interest in costs, numbers and finance. I earned my Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) in 2016.

  2. 02Nov 2016 – Sep 2019

    I became a banker.

    ICICI Bank. Privilege Banker.

    Banking was a very random decision after my graduation. I did financial analysis and MI reporting for risk management. I ran AML monitoring and KYC reviews on client portfolios.

    I observed the bank's products and applications, and thought about how to improve the experience for customers. I have seen things from the user's side since then.

  3. 03Dec 2019 – Sep 2020

    I moved to HDFC Bank.

    HDFC Bank. Personal Banker.

    I managed client portfolios and gave personal financial advice. I left to pursue my MBA.

  4. 042023

    I studied finance and big data.

    FORE School of Management, Delhi, India.

    During my MBA I interned in corporate finance at PwC India and in channel sales at Credflow.

    I completed my PGDM in Finance and Big Data Analytics in 2023.

  5. 05Aug 2023 – Present

    I became an investigator.

    PwC Acceleration Centers, Bangalore, India. Experienced Associate.

    I lead workstreams in fraud investigations. I manage and mentor a team of 8. This work taught me to pay attention to detail, find hidden patterns and structures, and think about how the client will find the work helpful.

    I convinced a healthcare client to give us a risk-screening dashboard project. I designed the dashboard and helped the team develop it. It cut the time to process a case from 30 to 10 minutes. This was product work, and I realised I enjoyed it a lot.

    Since 2024 I am a member of the Staff Advisory Council at PwC AC.

  6. 06Now

    I build AI products.

    After the dashboard, I decided to move into product and explore that area. I chose AI products because even people from non-tech fields can get hands-on and make products. That boosts my confidence.

    Then I did the AI Product Management course (8th cohort) by Shravan Tickoo at Rethink Systems, Jul – Sep 2026.

    I built and launched Adhikaar, a web app that guides families through bank-deposit claims.

    I built and launched Vantage, an AI learning app for consultants who work with clients.
